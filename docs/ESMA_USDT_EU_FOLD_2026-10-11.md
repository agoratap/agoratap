# EU stablecoin fold — 11 October 2026

Product and docs only. No custody build, no keys, no funds, no mainnet, and no ask of a principal.

**Pay-in: anything, including USDT on any chain.** Payers pay in whatever they hold, including USDT on any chain (Solana, Ethereum, Tron and others). XMR (Monero) is accepted where a swap route supports it (LibertySwap / Trocador-class path). Merchants receive any asset on any chain the swap route supports. EU merchants exclude only USDT (ESMA) and privacy coins like XMR (EU AMLR bars them at CASPs from July 2027); non-EU merchants are unrestricted. The ESMA restriction applies to the EU merchant payout leg only.

**Source.** ESMA Opinion of 8 October 2026 (ESMA75-113276571-1742). It tells EU crypto-asset service providers to stop services on non-MiCA stablecoins in the USDT class. National competent authorities’ wind-down runs to 8 January 2027.

**Legal.** Tom Hagen owns the legal read. This note is not a legal opinion.

**Unchanged.** Path A and partner fiat-out framing stay as they are. This repository does not rebuild CASP or custody. OFAC snapshot labels that happen to say USDT are sanctions-list text, not a payout rail.
