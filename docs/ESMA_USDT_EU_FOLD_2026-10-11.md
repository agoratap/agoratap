# EU stablecoin fold — 11 October 2026

Product and docs only. No custody build, no keys, no funds, no mainnet, and no ask of a principal.

**Pay-in: anything, including USDT on any chain.** Payers pay in whatever they hold, including USDT on any chain (Solana, Ethereum, Tron and others). XMR (Monero) is accepted where a swap route supports it (LibertySwap / Trocador-class path). Merchants receive any asset on any chain the swap route supports. EU merchants: direct transfer in the merchant's asset or practice mode for every asset; real Exolix, MAYAChain and THORChain swaps are not available for EU yet (a MiCA-authorised partner is coming), and payouts exclude USDT (ESMA) and privacy coins like XMR (EU AMLR bars them at CASPs from July 2027). Non-EU merchants: subject to local law and sanctions. The ESMA restriction applies to the EU merchant payout leg. Update 17 October 2026: the app does not create an Exolix order and does not request a MAYAChain or THORChain route for an EU merchant.

**Source.** ESMA Opinion of 8 October 2026 (ESMA75-113276571-1742). It tells EU crypto-asset service providers to stop services on non-MiCA stablecoins in the USDT class. National competent authorities’ wind-down runs to 8 January 2027.

**Legal.** Tom Hagen owns the legal read. This note is not a legal opinion.

**Unchanged.** Path A and partner fiat-out framing stay as they are. This repository does not rebuild CASP or custody. OFAC snapshot labels that happen to say USDT are sanctions-list text, not a payout rail.
